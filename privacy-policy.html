Privacy Policy

Twine · Last updated: September 9, 2026

Twine is a contact-book app that attaches AI call tools (accent conversion, live translation, transcript, captions) to your contacts. This policy explains what the app does with your data.

What Twine reads from your device

  • Contacts — with your permission, Twine reads names and photos from your device address book so it can show them next to calls. This stays on your device; it is not uploaded anywhere.
  • Phone number — used only to sign you in (a one-time code is sent to confirm the number is yours).

What Twine stores

Your contacts, the AI tools you've attached to each one, and your sign-in session are stored locally on your device. If your account is connected to our servers, the app mirrors your phone number and which tools are enabled per contact so the setting can follow your account — never your contacts' names, photos, or any call content. Our servers are never the source of truth for your contact list; the copy on your device always wins.

Call audio

Twine does not record, transmit, or store audio from your phone calls. Apple's iOS does not give any app access to system call audio or call state — Twine cannot listen to or capture your calls even if it wanted to. The AI call tools shown in the app run as a demonstration of what they would do; no call audio leaves your device.

What Twine does not do

  • No advertising or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No tracking across other apps or websites.

Your choices

You can revoke Contacts access at any time in your device Settings. Deleting the app removes everything stored locally. To delete your account and any data mirrored to our servers, contact us using the details below.
