Frequently asked

Why does sign-in ask for a US phone number?

Twine numbers are the identity behind the app — the same number your contacts already have you saved under. A code is texted to confirm the number is yours.

What are the AI call enhancers?

Accent Converter, Live Translator, Call Transcript, and Live Captions — tools you can attach to any contact. They can be turned on or off per contact, or set as a default for every new contact from the Enhancers tab, and can be toggled live during a call.

Does Twine listen to my calls?

No. iOS does not allow any app to access system call audio, and Twine does not request or use that access. See our Privacy Policy for details.

How do I remove a contact from Twine?

Open the contact's card and use the delete action. This only removes the card and its enhancer settings from Twine — the contact stays in your device address book.
