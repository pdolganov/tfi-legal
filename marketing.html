Twine

A contact book with AI call enhancers.

In testing on TestFlight — coming to the App Store soon

Twine keeps your address book exactly as it is — names, numbers and photos read straight from your device — and remembers how each person should be heard, every time they call.

Five ways to be understood better

Attach any combination of these to a contact once. The same tools switch on automatically the next time that contact calls, and can still be turned on or off mid-call.

  • Accent Converter — your voice is re-synthesized in real time with a different accent. The other side hears the converted voice.
  • Live Translator — real-time speech-to-speech translation. You both speak your own language.
  • Call Transcript — the whole conversation is transcribed and saved to your notebook after the call.
  • Live Captions — on-screen captions of what the other person is saying, as they speak.
  • Second Brain — reads the call along with you: terms, figures and claims are checked against what you hold, and answered while the sentence is still in the air.

Set once, per person

Enhancers can be set as a default for every contact, or overridden for one — an explicit choice for a single person always wins over the default. Nothing is sent off-device: Twine's own copy of your contacts is always the source of truth, never a server.
